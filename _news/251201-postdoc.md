A joint postdoctoral position is available at the Italian Institute of Technology (Genova, Italy) between our *Atomistic Simulations* unit and the *Computational Statistics and Machine Learning* group led by Massimiliano Pontil (co-director of the Genoa ELLIS Unit). 

The project, in collaboration with Pietro Novelli, will focus on representation learning for atomistic systems, including topics such as transfer learning and self-supervised learning of molecular representations. 

Information and application: [link](https://app.ncoreplat.com/jobposition/766671/1-joint-post-doctoral-position-in-atomistic-simulations-and-machine-learning-it/istituto-italiano-di-tecnologia)
